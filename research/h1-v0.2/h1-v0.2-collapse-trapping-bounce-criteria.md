# H1 v0.2 — аналитические критерии collapse, trapping и bounce для начальных данных тест регулярных начальных данных

## Критический итог

Из начального семейства тест регулярных начальных данных можно строго вывести существование гладкого одноконцевого 5D среза и, при `Q L²<1`, отсутствие **начальных trapped/anti-trapped круглых `S³`**. Из тех же данных нельзя вывести, что они в будущем коллапсируют: срез time-symmetric (`K_IJ=0`, `π_φ=0`), а его будущее не интегрировалось и не следует из constraints. Канонический massless scalar удовлетворяет bulk NEC, поэтому при последующем образовании trapped `S³` теорема Пенроуза в её подходящей 5D глобальной постановке даёт null geodesic incompleteness при выполнении всех глобальных гипотез. Это не доказывает кривизненный blow-up на конкретной координатной поверхности, не исключает всякое продолжение с нарушением гипотез и не создаёт дочернюю область.

В модели тест регулярных начальных данных нет потенциала ложного вакуума, стенки, brane, daughter FLRW congruence или transition event. Поэтому локальный bounce, новый 4D мир, сохранение внешней массы и `dt_D/dT_P` остаются **NOT_DERIVED**. Это аналитическая проверка ограниченных условий, не новое численное исследование.

## 1. Точная исходная модель и контроль размерностей

Воспроизводится именно family из [тест регулярных начальных данных](h1-v0.2-regular-initial-data-birth-test.md): 5D Einstein gravity плюс канонический massless scalar, `Λ₅=V=0`; сигнатура `(-,+,+,+,+)`, `c=ℏ=1`, `[κ₅²]=L³`, `[φ]=L⁻³ᐟ²`. Пространственный срез `Σ≅R⁴` имеет один asymptotically flat end, регулярный центр и conformally flat metric

\[
d\ell_4^2=\gamma_{IJ}dx^Idx^J=\psi(r)^2\bigl(dr^2+r^2d\Omega_3^2\bigr),
\quad K_{IJ}=0,\quad \pi_\phi=0,
\]

где `I,J=1,…,4`, `π_φ=n^A∂_Aφ` — нормальная плотность импульса на единицу proper volume, `P_φ=√γπ_φ` — canonical coordinate density. Исходный профиль и определения:

\[
q(r)=Q\frac{r^2}{L^2}e^{-r^2/L^2},\quad [Q]=L^{-2},\ [L]=L,
\]

\[
I(r)=\int_0^r s^3q(s)ds,\qquad
\psi(r)=1+\int_r^\infty\frac{I(x)}{x^3}dx,\qquad
\phi'(r)=\sqrt{\frac{6q(r)}{\kappa_5^2\psi(r)}}.
\]

`[q]=L⁻²`, `[I]=L²`, `ψ` dimensionless, `[φ′]=L⁻⁵ᐟ²`; hence `QL²` is dimensionless. The field is nonconstant but monotone, and has no double-well potential or wall. `φ′=O(r)` at the center, so the radial scalar extends smoothly as an even function on `R⁴`.

### Независимая проверка constraint identity

With the тест регулярных начальных данных convention `K_IJ=−γ_I{}^Aγ_J{}^B∇_An_B`, define `ρ=T_ABn^An^B`. In 5D the constraints are

\[
{}^{(4)}R+K^2-K_{IJ}K^{IJ}=2\kappa_5^2\rho+2\Lambda_5,
\qquad
D_J(K^J{}_I-\delta^J{}_IK)=\kappa_5^2j_I.
\]

For `γ_IJ=ψ²δ_IJ` in four spatial dimensions, `{}^{(4)}R=−6ψ⁻³Δ_{R⁴}ψ`. Since `ψ′=−I/r³`, `Δψ=−q`, so `R=6qψ⁻³`. Meanwhile `ρ=½ψ⁻²(φ′)²=3q/(κ₅²ψ³)`. Thus `2κ₅²ρ=6qψ⁻³=R`, exactly; momentum is zero because `K=π_φ=0`. This independently checks the nontrivial Hamiltonian identity and dimensions. It verifies only the initial slice.

## 2. 5D SO(4) symmetry, areal radius and null expansions

Any spherically symmetric 5D spacetime away from a center can locally be written

\[
ds_5^2=g_{ab}(x)dx^adx^b+R(x)^2d\Omega_3^2,
\]

where `a,b=0,1` span the Lorentzian orbit space and `R` is areal radius. Let `u^A` be a future unit timelike vector normal to a chosen spacelike slice, and `s^A` an outward unit radial spacelike vector, `u·u=−1`, `s·s=+1`, `u·s=0`. Fix the future null normalization

\[
k_+^A=u^A+s^A\quad(\text{outgoing}),\qquad
k_-^A=u^A-s^A\quad(\text{ingoing}),\qquad k_+\cdot k_-=-2.
\]

For round `S³` area `A_3=Ω_3R³`, `Ω_3=2π²`, the expansions are

\[
\theta_\pm=\frac{1}{A_3}\mathcal L_{k_\pm}A_3
=\frac{3}{R}k_\pm(R)
=\frac{3}{R}\left(u(R)\pm s(R)\right).
\]

The scale of `k_±` rescales the magnitudes but not the signs or marginal/trapped classification. A future trapped round sphere has both expansions negative; anti-trapped has both positive. A marginal sphere has one future expansion zero. Define the orbit-space invariant

\[
\chi=(\nabla R)^2=-u(R)^2+s(R)^2.
\]

Then `θ_+θ_- = −9χ/R²`. Thus `χ<0` is necessary for either future trapped or anti-trapped round spheres; the signs of `u(R)` distinguish the two. This invariant is safer than a coordinate lapse or a static-coordinate `f` near a horizon.

For тест регулярных начальных данных, `R(r)=rψ(r)`, proper radial distance `dℓ=ψdr`, and time symmetry gives `u(R)=0`. Hence

\[
\theta_\pm(0,r)=\pm\frac{3}{R}\frac{dR}{d\ell},\qquad
\chi(0,r)=\left(\frac{dR}{d\ell}\right)^2.
\]

The established bound `QL²<1` yields `dR/dℓ>0`, so initial round spheres are untrapped. Since `ψ′≤0`, also `dR/dℓ≤1`; the local geometric mass parameter below is nonnegative on this slice. None of these signs specifies the future `u(R)`.

## 3. 5D mass and what the initial compactness bound says

For spherical 5D Einstein gravity, define the geometric Misner–Sharp parameter

\[
\mu_{MS}=R^2\left[1-(\nabla R)^2\right]=R^2(1-\chi),\qquad [\mu_{MS}]=L^2.
\]

In a vacuum asymptotically flat Tangherlini region, `f(R)=1−μ/R²`, and this parameter equals the Tangherlini mass parameter. The physical mass convention is

\[
M=\frac{(5-3)\Omega_3}{16\pi G_5}\mu
=\frac{3\Omega_3}{16\pi G_5}\mu
=\frac{3\pi^2}{\kappa_5^2}\mu,
\quad \Omega_3=2\pi^2,\quad \kappa_5^2=8\pi G_5.
\]

With nonzero `Λ₅`, the vacuum metric convention `f=1−μ/R²−Λ₅R²/6` motivates a Λ-subtracted parameter `μ_Λ=R²(1−χ)−Λ₅R⁴/6`; with a nonconstant scalar, neither parameter is generally constant. The invariant trapping criterion remains `χ<0` and does not require calling it mass.

On the time-symmetric тест регулярных начальных данных slice,

\[
\frac{\mu_{MS}}{R^2}=1-\left(\frac{dR}{d\ell}\right)^2.
\]

Under `QL²<1`, `0<dR/dℓ≤1`, so `0≤μ_MS/R²<1` for finite `r>0`: no initial marginal/trapped round sphere. At infinity `ψ=1+QL⁴/(2r²)+o(r^{-2})`, so the asymptotic Tangherlini parameter is `μ_∞=2QL⁴`, and the тест регулярных начальных данных ADM mass is `3Ω₃μ_∞/(16πG₅)`. This is positive finite energy, not a proof of collapse.

The condition `QL²<1` is a sufficient initial no-trapping bound derived for that exact profile. It does **not** specify an analytic threshold separating future dispersion from black-hole formation. Small initial compactness alone is not a theorem of dispersion for the full nonlinear 5D evolution; a critical `Q L²`, if any for this family, requires evolution and convergence evidence.

## 4. NEC, Raychaudhuri focusing and singularity scope

For the canonical scalar stress tensor and any bulk null vector,

\[
T_{AB}k^Ak^B=(k^A\nabla_A\phi)^2\ge0.
\]

So the 5D bulk scalar obeys NEC independent of the sign of `V(φ)`. A cosmological term and the scalar-potential term are proportional to `G_AB`, hence vanish in null contraction. A negative bulk `Λ₅` changes asymptotics and global boundary assumptions, but does not itself reverse null convergence in the Einstein equation. WEC is a separate condition: it holds pointwise for the canonical scalar when `V≥0`, but need not for sufficiently negative potentials. SEC is different again; a positive vacuum potential can violate SEC while satisfying NEC. A positive pure-tension surface has surface NEC saturated (`ρ+p=0`) and WEC for `σ≥0`; its distributional focusing requires the Israel jump, not a smooth-fluid substitution.

For a hypersurface-orthogonal null congruence in `D=5`, the screen dimension is three and Raychaudhuri is

\[
\frac{d\theta}{d\lambda}=-\frac{\theta^2}{3}-\sigma_{AB}\sigma^{AB}-R_{AB}k^Ak^B
\quad (\omega_{AB}=0).
\]

Under the stated canonical Einstein–scalar equations, `R_AB k^A k^B=κ₅²(k·∇φ)²≥0`; thus a twist-free congruence focuses. For spherical radial congruences shear also vanishes. This supports focusing/caustic or trapped-surface formation once the corresponding expansion is negative; it does not force every profile to collapse, determine an event horizon, or show a bounce.

A higher-dimensional Penrose-type conclusion applies only if the selected theorem's hypotheses hold, including a globally hyperbolic spacetime/domain, a noncompact Cauchy surface, a compact future trapped surface and null convergence. If a future trapped `S³` forms in a regular evolution of this one-ended asymptotically flat model and those global assumptions remain valid, the conclusion is future null geodesic incompleteness. It does not by itself prove that curvature diverges at a specific point, that no extension exists under any altered assumptions, or that an expanding daughter emerges. Cosmic censorship is not assumed. An apparent/marginally trapped surface is slice-local; an event horizon is the global boundary of the causal past of a chosen future infinity.

## 5. Can one infer collapse, bounce or a daughter from тест регулярных начальных данных?

### 5.1 Collapse and trapped-surface formation

The тест регулярных начальных данных data have `K_IJ=0` and zero scalar normal momentum. They are instantaneously time-symmetric, not an explicitly inward-moving collapse slice. Positive ADM mass and a nonzero scalar gradient can source gravitational evolution, but the constraints alone do not fix whether the chosen future branch disperses or collapses. To establish collapse one must evolve the Einstein–scalar initial-value problem and track `u(R)`, `χ`, scalar flux and regularity; no such calculation is part of this report.

The precise future condition for a round future-trapped sphere is

\[
u(R)<-|s(R)|\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \theta_+<0\text{ and }\theta_-<0.
\]

This is a necessary local trapping diagnostic in the chosen foliation, not a criterion for a new universe. If `χ<0` but `u(R)>|s(R)|`, the sphere is anti-trapped instead. A marginal tube where one expansion vanishes is an apparent/trapping horizon candidate; its causal character and global relation to event horizons still need the evolved metric.

### 5.2 Bounce criteria

For an areal-radius shell/congruence, a local regular bounce requires `u(R)=0` at a positive finite `R_b`, `d[u(R)]/dτ>0` along the selected future timelike flow, and finite curvature/invariant stress. Equivalently, its expansion changes from negative to positive along that flow. For a genuinely homogeneous 4D daughter, the distinct criterion is `H_D=(1/a_D)da_D/dt_D=0`, `dH_D/dt_D>0` plus regular 4D invariants. тест регулярных начальных данных contains no such daughter congruence or `a_D`.

The flat-FLRW canonical-scalar result `dot H=−κ φdot²/2≤0` (with its dimension-dependent normalization) is not a general theorem for inhomogeneous 5D geometry, curved FLRW slices, wall embeddings or a chosen non-cosmological congruence. Positive spatial curvature can alter a Friedmann bounce condition; a wall-radius bounce is an embedding effect; neither is automatically a daughter cosmological bounce. Introducing a phantom scalar merely to obtain `dot H>0` changes the kinetic sign and introduces a ghost; it is not an admissible inference from тест регулярных начальных данных.

NEC focusing plus an already trapped sphere and Penrose hypotheses constrains future completeness; it is not a theorem that every possible bounce in every extension is impossible. A nonsingular bounce would need a complete solution satisfying the chosen equations and showing how its congruences avoid the focusing conclusion (for example, by violating a theorem hypothesis, changing causal/global conditions, or using a different gravitational action). No such solution is exhibited here.

## 6. Parent exterior, mass and separate clocks

In 5D spherical vacuum Einstein gravity with fixed `Λ₅`, Birkhoff/Tangherlini implies a local vacuum exterior `f=1−μ/R²−Λ₅R²/6` with constant parameter in that vacuum domain. The тест регулярных начальных данных scalar profile has decaying but nonzero tails, so one must not call a finite-radius region exactly vacuum merely from large `r`. In a true vacuum exterior separated from scalar support, the local mass parameter is constant; the ADM charge is conserved only for suitable asymptotics and no flux through the defining boundary. During scalar propagation, bulk gravitational radiation, a brane/interface, or projected Weyl stresses, local quasi-local mass/horizon geometry can change. No universal “mass transferred to daughter” law follows.

`T_P` is proper time on a specified parent observer; `τ_transition` is proper time of a specified wall/bounce worldline if one exists; `t_D` is daughter FLRW proper time only if a homogeneous induced daughter geometry exists; `t_5` is a bulk coordinate. Comparing `T_P` and `t_D` requires emission/reception events, causal bulk path or mediator, observer worldlines and a clock protocol. The static pre-existing RS1 ratio `e^{-kL}` is not a dynamic newborn-daughter mapping. `dt_D/dT_P=K0a_D` remains **NOT_DERIVED**.

## 8. Skeptical conclusion and falsification gates

**Skeptical conclusion:** тест регулярных начальных данных is stronger than a verbal proposal because it gives exact nontrivial regular 5D scalar initial data, and the `QL²<1` bound rules out an initially trapped round sphere for this family. It is weaker than a collapse result: the time-symmetric data may disperse, and there is no analytic future solution here. NEC focusing becomes consequential only after a trapped surface is actually established and all global theorem premises are checked. Neither a trapped surface, a singular endpoint nor a local shell/field domain alone demonstrates a new expanding daughter.

Acceptance gates for any future claim: (1) solve/evolve the same 5D action from declared regular constraints; (2) independently verify null expansions and curvature with convergence; (3) if a wall forms, verify its timelike nondegenerate induced metric and source/junction equations; (4) construct the relevant global causal domain and test signals to a named parent observer/infinity; (5) define any daughter FLRW congruence and clock comparison from actual events; (6) show stable positive-kinetic modes and a controlled exterior mass/flux balance. Failure of a gate rejects that specific candidate, not every H1 variant.

## 9. Ровно один ограниченный следующий теоретический вопрос

Для **той же** massless profile семьи тест регулярных начальных данных аналитически получить эволюционный знак `u(R)` в контролируемом weak-field expansion, если такой expansion существует при `QL²≪1`, и определить, можно ли доказать дисперсию либо только кратковременный контроль до arrival at the regular center. До этого нужно вывести соответствующую SO(4)-reduced Einstein–scalar evolution and error-free asymptotic assumptions. Stop, если нелинейные члены/глобальная радиационная boundary problem не позволяют строгую оценку; не подменять её численной иллюстрацией.
