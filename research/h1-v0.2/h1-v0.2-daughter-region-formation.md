# H1 v0.2 — может ли новая 4D область динамически возникнуть в общем 5D bulk?

## Краткий вывод

5D Einstein gravity допускает timelike 4D hypersurface с индуцированной Lorentzian метрикой; wall/branes могут иметь собственные observers, scale factor и proper time. При заданных bulk initial/boundary data гравитационные возмущения способны распространяться между уже существующими branes причинными путями bulk. Это **не показывает**, что новая brane или отдельный дочерний мир возникли из регулярного родительского среза.

Минимальный скалярный доменный дефект может иметь 3+1-мерный worldvolume. Его динамическое появление требует фазовой структуры потенциала и подходящих начальных данных/неустойчивости/флуктуаций; точное вакуумное решение классически не начинает распадаться само. Тонкая оболочка ещё сильнее предполагает заранее заданный интерфейс. Ни wall expansion, ни его bounce не меняют автоматически топологию bulk и не доказывают отдельную глобальную causal component.

Таким образом, в ограниченной 5D модели **условно возможно** получить расширяющуюся индуцированную 4D геометрию на доменной стенке; образование такой стенки из regular parent-only данных, её интерпретация как отдельной дочерней вселенной и связь `dt_D/dT_P` остаются **NOT_DERIVED**. Аналитического полного collapse solution здесь нет; симуляции не проводились.

«Новая область» далее означает либо (a) field-defined wall worldvolume, либо (b) отдельный открытый bulk region. Эти объекты геометрически различны. Доказательство одного не считается доказательством другого.

## 2. A — false-vacuum wall и 5D Israel thin shell

### 2.1 Действие и размерности

В сигнатуре `(-,+,+,+,+)`, при `c=ℏ=1`, пусть 5D vacuum по разные стороны wall имеет effective cosmological constants `Λ_±`. На каждом bulk patch

\[
S_{bulk,\pm}=\frac{1}{2\kappa_5^2}\int_{M_\pm}d^5x\sqrt{-g_5}(R_5-2\Lambda_\pm)
+\frac{1}{\kappa_5^2}\int_{\partial M_\pm}d^4x\sqrt{-h}\,K_\pm,
\]

а тонкая timelike shell `Σ` имеет action

\[
S_\Sigma=-\sigma\int_\Sigma d^4\xi\sqrt{-h}+S_{m,\Sigma}[h,\psi_\Sigma].
\]

`[κ₅²]=L³`, `[Λ_±]=L⁻²`, `[σ]=L⁻⁴`; `h_ab` — pullback, `K_ab` — extrinsic curvature с объявленной outward normal. The GHY terms are for the cut bulk faces; both sides must be varied with a single stated jump convention. Wall stress is `S_ab=−σ h_ab+τ_ab`, so positive constant tension has surface `ρ=σ`, `p=−σ`.

The distributional 5D Einstein equation and Israel condition are

\[
G^{(5)}_{AB}+\Lambda G_{AB}=\kappa_5^2 T^{bulk}_{AB}
\quad (\text{away from }\Sigma),\qquad
[K_{ab}-K h_{ab}]=-\kappa_5^2 S_{ab}.
\]

This is a **5D** jump: shell is 4D and the coefficient is `κ₅²`, not the 4D `8πG₄` coefficient from experiment #33. The induced wall stress conservation is

\[
D^a S_{ab}=-[T^{bulk}_{AB}n^A e^B{}_b].
\]

It is conserved intrinsically if the bulk has no tangential flux. Normal projection plus variation of the embedding supplies the wall acceleration equation; it must not be replaced by an externally prescribed `R(τ)`.

### 2.2 Локальный пример и wall FLRW часы

For a 5D spherically symmetric vacuum patch with 3-sphere sections,

\[
ds_\pm^2=-f_\pm(R)dT_\pm^2+\frac{dR^2}{f_\pm(R)}+R^2d\Omega_3^2,
\qquad
f_\pm(R)=1-\frac{\mu_\pm}{R^2}-\frac{\Lambda_\pm R^2}{6}.
\]

Here `[μ]=L²`; `Λ_eff=Λ₅+κ₅²V_vac` if a homogeneous vacuum energy `V_vac` is placed in the bulk scalar sector. For wall trajectory `R(τ_Σ)`, induced metric is

\[
ds_\Sigma^2=-d\tau_\Sigma^2+R(\tau_\Sigma)^2d\Omega_3^2,
\qquad a_\Sigma(\tau_\Sigma)=R(\tau_\Sigma)/R_*.
\]

Thus a **pre-existing** spherical timelike wall has a closed-FLRW-type 3+1 worldvolume. It is a cosmological geometry on the wall only if matter/gravity degrees of freedom are actually localized there. Thin-wall tension alone does not prove localized 4D gravity. In the static bulk chart, where `f_±>0`, proper-time normalization gives

\[
f_\pm\dot T_\pm^2-\frac{\dot R^2}{f_\pm}=1,
\qquad
\dot T_\pm=\frac{\sqrt{\dot R^2+f_\pm}}{f_\pm}
\]

for the forward static-time branch. The latter coordinate formula is chart-limited; the wall proper time and induced metric remain the physical local description at a regular horizon crossing.

Let `β_±=ε_±√(dot R²+f_±)`, where `ε_±=sign(n^A∂_A R)` encodes the orientation of the normal radial derivative, not a global topology label. For pure tension, the angular Israel equation is, under the convention `[Q]=Q_+−Q_-`,

\[
\epsilon_-\sqrt{\dot R^2+f_-}-\epsilon_+\sqrt{\dot R^2+f_+}
=\frac{\kappa_5^2\sigma}{3}R\equiv qR.
\]

The factor `1/3` is `1/(D−2)` for a 4D wall in 5D; it is not the 4D spherical-shell coefficient used in #33. Squaring once yields a convenient local radial equation

\[
\epsilon_+\sqrt{\dot R^2+f_+}
=\frac{f_-−f_+−q^2R^2}{2qR},\qquad
\dot R^2=W(R)=\left(\frac{f_-−f_+−q^2R^2}{2qR}\right)^2-f_+(R).
\]

This `W` is only a candidate effective potential: every point/root must also satisfy the original unsquared equation, both `ε` signs and real square roots. A regular wall-radius bounce requires `R_b>0`, `W(R_b)=0`, a locally allowed side `W≥0`, and `ddot R_b=W'(R_b)/2>0` for a smooth simple root. Its induced `H_Σ=dot R/R` then passes from negative to positive. A turnaround has the opposite acceleration. Neither result means the wall was created at `R_b`.

The shell trajectory provides a transparent conditional example of how intrinsic expansion arises from an embedding. It is not a complete parent-collapse history: a global bulk, regular initial slice and causal completion are still required. No parameter values are fit or numerically scanned here.

### 2.3 Начальные данные и образование

A thin shell is distributional initial data. If present on the initial Cauchy surface, it is a prepared defect, not a formation result. Smooth “parent-only” data have regular `g_AB`, extrinsic curvature and matter satisfying the 5D Hamiltonian/momentum constraints, specified asymptotic end(s), and no initially inserted shell/throat/second region. The thin-wall action has no field whose zero set can nucleate from those data: it already assumes `Σ` exists.

An Israel solution therefore answers “which local matching histories are compatible with this wall stress and chosen bulk patches?”, not “does regular collapse dynamically make a new wall?” A formation calculation needs either a resolved wall field, or a covariant defect/nucleation mechanism and its energy budget, plus the full constraint-satisfying initial-value problem.

## 3. B — smooth 5D scalar defect and topological wall

### 3.1 Ковариантная модель

A minimal thick-wall theory is

\[
S=\frac{1}{2\kappa_5^2}\int_B\sqrt{-G}(R_5-2\Lambda_5)
-\int_B\sqrt{-G}\left[\frac12G^{AB}\partial_A\Phi\partial_B\Phi+V(\Phi)\right]
+S_{GHY}.
\]

`[Φ]=L⁻³ᐟ²`, `[V]=L⁻⁵`; the field equations are

\[
G_{AB}+\Lambda_5G_{AB}=\kappa_5^2T_{AB},\quad
T_{AB}=\partial_A\Phi\partial_B\Phi-G_{AB}\left[\frac12(\nabla\Phi)^2+V\right],\quad
\Box_5\Phi-V'(\Phi)=0.
\]

The contracted Bianchi identity plus scalar equation gives `∇^A T_AB=0`; total energy is not lost into a brane by definition. In a brane-forming solution the localized stress is a smooth part of `T_AB`, and the thin-wall source/junction appears only as an approximation after integrating across its thickness.

For a degenerate double well, take

\[
V(\Phi)=\frac{\lambda}{4}(\Phi^2-v^2)^2,\qquad \lambda>0,\ v>0,
\]

with `[λ]=L`, `[v]=L⁻³ᐟ²`. In the **nongravitating flat-wall limit** an exact kink is

\[
\Phi(y)=v\tanh(y/\delta),\qquad
\delta=\frac{\sqrt2}{v\sqrt\lambda},\qquad
\sigma=\int dy\left[\frac12(\Phi')^2+V\right]=\frac{2\sqrt{2\lambda}}{3}v^3.
\]

This confirms that the scalar theory can carry a smooth finite-tension wall between distinct vacua. It is not an exact gravitating-collapse solution. With gravity, the warp factor and scalar profile must jointly solve Einstein–scalar equations, e.g. a static ansatz `ds²=e^{2A(y)}η_{μν}dx^μdx^ν+dy²` leads to coupled ODEs and boundary/asymptotic conditions; `A(y)` cannot be chosen freely. A nondegenerate false/true vacuum energy difference adds pressure on the wall and changes its motion; it does not remove the need for initial data.

### 3.2 Когда level set является worldvolume

Define a candidate wall locally by `Φ(X)=Φ_*`. At regular points, `∇_AΦ≠0`. Its normal is spacelike and the level set timelike only if

\[
G^{AB}\partial_A\Phi\partial_B\Phi>0.
\]

Then the pullback metric has Lorentzian signature `(-,+,+,+)` (for a regular embedded level set). If the gradient is timelike, the level set is spacelike; if null/zero, the worldvolume degenerates and is not a 4D timelike brane. A field threshold alone is insufficient: one must check this signature condition, curvature, finite thickness and localization of matter/gravitational modes.

A kink interpolating between disconnected vacua is topologically protected only with appropriate boundary conditions/topological sector (for example distinct vacua at opposite ends of a noncompact transverse direction). A finite closed bubble in the same exterior vacuum at both sides is not automatically topologically protected; it may collapse. In 5 spatial dimensions a planar wall has infinite total energy unless the transverse/brane directions are compact or energy is considered per unit 3-volume; a closed 3-sphere wall has finite energy but is dynamical and may contract. These are distinct boundary-value problems.

For a canonical scalar and null `k^A`,

\[
T_{AB}k^Ak^B=(k^A\partial_A\Phi)^2\ge0.
\]

Thus the bulk scalar obeys NEC. For timelike unit `u^A`, `T_ABu^Au^B=\frac12(u·∂Φ)^2+\frac12(\nabla_\perp Φ)^2+V`; WEC is nonnegative if `V≥0`. A positive-tension thin wall has surface NEC saturated (`ρ+p=0`) and WEC for `σ≥0`; its surface SEC is violated because `ρ+3p=−2σ`. SEC violation alone is not a construction of a bounce. Bulk cosmological constant drops out of null contraction; energy conditions for effective brane equations must include projected Weyl/brane terms rather than be inferred from bare wall tension.

Classical formation is not spontaneous in an exactly symmetric vacuum solution: uniqueness of the deterministic field evolution keeps exact vacuum data in that vacuum. A tachyonic/unstable phase can amplify a specified perturbation; a finite-amplitude smooth initial profile can form domains; thermal/quantum nucleation needs additional physics/statistics. A field profile that already crosses the barrier or contains a bubble is a seed and must be described as such. A topological defect changes the matter configuration, not automatically the topology of the 5D manifold.

### 3.3 Localized gravity is a separate requirement

A scalar wall gives localized energy and a geometric 4D level set. It does **not** by itself localize a normalizable massless 4D graviton or confine Standard Model fields. That requires a demonstrated mode spectrum, compactification/warping or explicit brane terms and healthy kinetic norms. Without it, `h_ab` is the induced metric on a defect, not necessarily a self-contained 4D gravitational universe. If matter is added only to the wall, its conservation follows from the full 5D equations and absence of normal flux; allowing bulk fields to cross the wall produces a projected flux term.

## 4. C — related mechanisms and why they are not the requested birth

- **Black string / black brane:** a 5D horizon extended along a brane direction is a solution with pre-existing horizon topology. Its interior/horizon may have nontrivial causal structure, but it is not evidence that a new 4D worldvolume nucleated from smooth parent-only data. A 4D brane exterior need not be Schwarzschild because projected bulk Weyl curvature can source it.
- **Collision of pre-existing branes:** a collision can convert bulk/brane kinetic energy into localized matter and alter induced cosmology. Both branes and their embeddings are already part of the initial data; collision is not brane creation. A singular collision or bounce needs its own regularization/action.
- **Bubble of nothing / quantum vacuum decay:** these require compactification/topological boundary data and generally a semiclassical saddle or instability. They are not derived from the classical wall equations above and no action/probability is computed here.

These are contrasts, not terms to add to the scalar-wall action. No torsion, HR bimetric potential or 4D #33 shell equation is grafted into the 5D model.

## 5. Causal structure, parent exterior and no-go scope

### 5.1 Causal and clock requirements

There is one 5D causal manifold `B` with bulk cones `J_B^±`. A brane’s intrinsic causal cones are induced by `h_i`; a bulk null ray may connect two branes even when no brane-confined matter travels between them. Such a connection is physical only if a bulk causal curve exists with the imposed boundary conditions. A wall observer’s proper time is `dτ_Σ²=−h_ab dξ^adξ^b` in `c=1`; for restored units `dτ_Σ=√(−h_ab dξ^adξ^b)/c` when coordinates carry length units. A parent brane observer has `T_P` from `h_P`; daughter candidate observer has `t_D` from an actual induced FLRW congruence if one exists.

Mapping `T_P↔t_D` requires a specified emission event/worldline on P, bulk causal path or propagating mode, reception event/worldline on D, and synchronization/clock protocol. There may be multiple rays/modes and hence multiple arrival maps. No universal `t_D=f(T_P)` or `dt_D/dT_P=K0a_D` follows from an embedding alone. In a disconnected-topology proposal with no bulk path/map, causal cross-influence vanishes unless a new mediator is defined; if the mediator couples the manifolds, its causal domain is part of the theory.

### 5.2 Может ли внешний parent BH сохраниться?

In vacuum, spherically symmetric 5D Einstein gravity gives a Tangherlini-(A)dS-type exterior with constant mass parameter in each vacuum region. A parent brane’s induced 4D exterior is not guaranteed to be Schwarzschild: the projected Weyl term, bulk gravitational radiation, brane bending, scalar stress and energy flux can change it. A familiar 4D Schwarzschild exterior is a conditional limit requiring negligible exterior brane matter, no relevant bulk/Weyl flux, appropriate low-energy GR and boundary conditions. Even then mass conservation depends on the chosen asymptotic charge and flux through the boundary/horizon. The action does not say parent mass is necessarily lost or necessarily unchanged.

### 5.3 Теоремы и точные границы применимости

- **Farhi–Guth (1987):** the cited obstacle concerns a particular classical 4D spherical, asymptotically flat false-vacuum bubble/NEC class and a sufficiently large expanding bubble under their hypotheses. It cannot be transferred as a theorem to 5D bulk plus brane without deriving the corresponding 5D global assumptions, energy conditions and asymptotic structure. This extension is **NOT_DERIVED** here.
- **Penrose singularity theorem:** requires a trapped surface, suitable global hyperbolicity/noncompact Cauchy assumptions and null convergence/focusing. It concludes geodesic incompleteness, not “a daughter universe forms.” In 5D with canonical scalar Einstein equations, bulk null convergence holds where the classical equations apply; thin-wall distribution and boundary/orbifold terms need separate treatment. Whether a candidate collapse satisfies all global hypotheses is not established.
- **Topological censorship:** requires global asymptotic and averaged-null-energy conditions on a globally hyperbolic spacetime. It constrains observable topology through a given infinity; it is not a universal ban on hidden brane regions or all 5D constructions. Check the actual bulk completion before applying it.
- **Global hyperbolicity/topology:** in a globally hyperbolic spacetime Cauchy surfaces are diffeomorphic; a claimed topology change therefore requires a failure of at least one relevant global assumption, a singular/incomplete evolution, or a more careful notion of changing effective region. This is not a license to assert topology change when those assumptions fail.

The shell/defect may obey NEC while positive vacuum potential violates strong energy condition; those are different statements. No single energy-condition label decides the global formation question. Quantum tunneling is a different process and is not analyzed as a classical counterexample.

## 7. Символические допустимые и запрещённые области

- **Bulk/brane:** `κ₅²>0`; `G_AB` nondegenerate Lorentzian; each embedding full rank and timelike; `h_i` signature `−+++`; normals unit spacelike and jump signs fixed. A candidate level-set brane additionally needs `∇Φ·∇Φ>0` and `∇Φ≠0`. Degenerate/null pullback is not a Lorentzian 4D universe.
- **Canonical scalar:** `V(Φ)≥0` is sufficient for nonnegative local scalar energy density, and `T_ABk^Ak^B≥0` for every null `k`. This supplies NEC but does not guarantee inflation/bounce or defect formation. A wall of positive tension has WEC and saturates surface NEC; negative tension is not silently allowed in the classical candidate.
- **Thin wall:** `σ>0`, `R>0`, real `√(dotR²+f_±)`, `W≥0`, and the **unsquared** Israel equation with the same `ε_±` at each event. Bounce requires `W=0` and `W'>0`; no root or wrong sign rejects that trajectory. Thin-wall regime requires wall thickness small versus curvature/radius scales, schematically `δ/R≪1` and `δ|K|≪1`; exact error bounds are model-dependent and not assigned.
- **Stable gravity:** positive 5D Einstein kinetic sign is necessary, not sufficient. All physical tensor, scalar/radion and wall-bending modes need positive kinetic norm, nonnegative gradient matrix and hyperbolic characteristics on a concrete background. No finite numerical parameter ranges are asserted.
- **Energy and exterior:** brane-local matter conservation requires zero normal/tangential bulk transfer in the relevant projection. Otherwise include the derived flux. A conserved parent asymptotic mass requires an asymptotic symmetry and controlled flux; it is not inferred from wall tension alone.

## 9. Вердикт и ровно один следующий вопрос

**Физически возможно внутри ограниченных игрушечных моделей:** существующая timelike wall имеет собственную индуцированную 4D геометрию; тонкостенная динамика может дать расширяющийся worldvolume при допустимом root/branch. Скалярное поле в 5D допускает smooth domain-wall sector при подходящем потенциале и данных. Эти результаты — существование локальной структуры/условный процесс, не empirical establishment H1.

**Не показано:** регулярное parent-only collapse формирует стенку; она несёт здоровую 4D гравитацию; появляется глобально отдельная daughter causal domain; внешний parent BH сохраняет заданную массу; существует универсальная связь часов. При общей связной 5D bulk wall regions могут обмениваться bulk сигналами. При переходе к действительно несвязным manifold этот канал исчезает, если не добавить новый mediator.

**Один следующий аналитический вопрос:** для фиксированного потенциала `V(Φ)=λ(Φ²−v²)²/4` вывести и проверить полный сферически-симметричный 5D Einstein–scalar Cauchy system с регулярным центром, одним asymptotically AdS/flat end (выбрать один и зафиксировать его), smooth initial data без wall crossing, constraints и NEC; определить, допускает ли constrained evolution появление timelike level-set wall до trapped-surface/gauge breakdown. Сначала решить вопрос начальных/граничных условий и well-posedness; не объявлять wall как готовую brane. Это аналитическая постановка, не разрешение на численный запуск.
