# H1 v0.2 — регулярные 5D начальные данные и причинное образование 4D области

## Краткий вывод

Для 5D Einstein–scalar с каноническим massless scalar построен ниже явный класс гладких, time-symmetric, асимптотически плоских одноконцевых начальных данных с ненулевым полем и **точно удовлетворёнными гамильтоновым и импульсным ограничениями**. При указанном достаточном symbolic bound начальные круглые `S³` не trapped. Эти данные не содержат brane, false-vacuum bubble, wall или второго конца.

Этот результат показывает, что регулярный parent-like scalar seed без заранее поставленной дочерней геометрии математически допустим. Он **не показывает**, что seed коллапсирует, создаёт defect, проходит регулярный bounce, образует причинно отделившуюся 4D область или задаёт собственные дочерние часы. Эволюция и глобальное продолжение не рассчитывались. Для точного вакуумного состояния классическая детерминированная динамика не nucleates scalar wall сама по себе.

В ограниченном смысле ответ на центральный вопрос: **начальные данные существуют; динамическое рождение дочерней области из них не выведено**. Ни локальный wall, ни trapped surface после эволюции сами по себе не доказывают новый manifold.

## 1. Теория, данные Коши и понятие регулярности

### 1.1 Действие и соглашения

Берём 5D Einstein gravity с одним каноническим скаляром, сигнатура `(-,+,+,+,+)`, `c=ℏ=1`:

\[
S=\frac{1}{2\kappa_5^2}\int_{\mathcal M_5}d^5x\sqrt{-G}(R_5-2\Lambda_5)
-\int_{\mathcal M_5}d^5x\sqrt{-G}\left[\frac12G^{AB}\nabla_A\phi\nabla_B\phi+V(\phi)\right]
+S_{GHY}+S_{\rm asymptotic}.
\]

`[κ₅²]=L³`, `[Λ₅]=L⁻²`, `[φ]=L⁻³ᐟ²`, `[V]=L⁻⁵`; `S` is dimensionless. `S_GHY` is included on any actual boundary/cut with stated outward normal; an asymptotically flat slice has the corresponding ADM boundary term/charge. For this explicit example there is no material boundary, brane, thin shell, or localized delta source. Choose `V=0`, `Λ₅=0`; the vacuum at infinity is then flat. More generally an asymptotically flat vacuum requires `Λ_eff=Λ₅+κ₅²V(φ_∞)=0`; asymptotically AdS requires `Λ_eff<0` plus AdS falloffs, not the flat boundary condition used below.

The covariant equations are

\[
G_{AB}+\Lambda_5G_{AB}=\kappa_5^2T_{AB},\qquad
T_{AB}=\nabla_A\phi\nabla_B\phi-G_{AB}\left[\frac12(\nabla\phi)^2+V\right],
\qquad \Box_5\phi-V'(\phi)=0.
\]

The scalar equation implies `∇^AT_AB=0`; the Einstein constraints are not optional independent equations.

### 1.2 4D spacelike Cauchy surface внутри 5D

Let `Σ` be a spacelike hypersurface of dimension **four** inside `M₅`, with induced spatial metric `γ_IJ=G_AB e^A_I e^B_J` and future unit normal `n^A`, `n·n=−1`; `I,J=1,…,4` label slice directions and `A,B=0,…,4` bulk directions. Use the sign convention

\[
K_{IJ}=-\gamma_I{}^A\gamma_J{}^B\nabla_An_B,
\quad K=\gamma^{IJ}K_{IJ},\quad
\pi_\phi=n^A\nabla_A\phi,
\]

and define `ρ=T_AB n^A n^B`, `j_I=−γ_I{}^A T_AB n^B`. `π_φ` is the normal momentum per proper spatial volume; the canonical coordinate density is `P_φ=√γ π_φ`. The 5D Einstein Hamiltonian and momentum constraints on `Σ` are

\[
{}^{(4)}R[\gamma]+K^2-K_{IJ}K^{IJ}=2\kappa_5^2\rho+2\Lambda_5,
\]

\[
D_J(K^J{}_I-\delta^J{}_I K)=\kappa_5^2j_I.
\]

For the canonical scalar,

\[
\rho=\frac12\left(\pi_\phi^2+D_I\phi D^I\phi\right)+V(\phi),
\qquad j_I=-\pi_\phi D_I\phi.
\]

These signs correspond to the stated `K_IJ` and `j_I` definitions; reversing the convention for `K` changes the momentum-constraint presentation. A 4D brane on `Σ` would be codimension one in 5D and require its own distributional source/Israel jump. It is **not** the same object as this Cauchy surface.

### 1.3 Что здесь означает regular parent-like data

The explicit slice has topology `Σ≅R⁴`, one asymptotically flat end and a regular spherical center. “Regular” means: `γ_IJ` positive-definite and smooth; `K_IJ,φ,π_φ` smooth with spherical parity at the center; finite curvature invariants and finite ADM energy; constraints satisfied; no conical defect, thin shell, pre-inserted disconnected component, throat or brane. We additionally test round trapped/anti-trapped `S³` at the initial slice. This is a candidate parent-only Cauchy surface, **not** a claim that it represents a pre-existing black-hole interior.

## 2. Exact nontrivial constrained initial-data family

This is a constraint construction, not a numerical evolution. Let `r` be isotropic radius on `R⁴`, `dΩ₃²` the unit 3-sphere metric, and choose a conformally flat spatial metric and time-symmetric data:

\[
d\ell_4^2=\gamma_{IJ}dx^Idx^J=\psi(r)^2\left(dr^2+r^2d\Omega_3^2\right),
\quad K_{IJ}=0,\quad \pi_\phi=0,
\quad \Lambda_5=V=0.
\]

Set `q(r)=Q(r²/L²)e^{-r²/L²}`, with `Q>0`, `[Q]=L⁻²`, `[L]=L`. Define

\[
I(r)=\int_0^r s^3q(s)\,ds,
\qquad
\psi(r)=1+\int_r^\infty\frac{I(x)}{x^3}\,dx,
\qquad
\phi(r)=\phi_0+\int_0^r\sqrt{\frac{6q(s)}{\kappa_5^2\psi(s)}}\,ds.
\]

Then `ψ'=-I/r³`, so the flat radial Laplacian in four spatial dimensions obeys

\[
\Delta_{\mathbb R^4}\psi=\psi''+\frac3r\psi'=-q(r).
\]

For `γ_IJ=ψ²δ_IJ` in `n=4` spatial dimensions the conformal-curvature identity is

\[
{}^{(4)}R[\gamma]=-6\psi^{-3}\Delta_{\mathbb R^4}\psi=6q\psi^{-3}.
\]

Also `D_IφD^Iφ=ψ^{-2}(φ')²`, and by construction

\[
2\kappa_5^2\rho=\kappa_5^2\psi^{-2}(\phi')^2=6q\psi^{-3}={}^{(4)}R[\gamma].
\]

Thus the Hamiltonian residual is **analytically zero**. Since `K_IJ=0` and `π_φ=0`, both sides of the momentum constraint vanish pointwise: its residual is **analytically zero**. This does not depend on a numerical discretization or on a solver tolerance.

### 2.1 Smoothness, asymptotics and symbolic no-trapped bound

Near `r=0`, `q(r)=Qr²/L²+O(r⁴)`, hence `I(r)=Qr⁶/(6L²)+O(r⁸)`, `ψ'(r)=O(r³)` and `φ'(r)=O(r)`. The radial functions therefore extend smoothly through the center as even functions of Cartesian radius. `ψ≥1` and `φ'` decays exponentially. Since

\[
I(\infty)=QL^4\int_0^\infty x^5e^{-x^2}dx=QL^4,
\quad
\psi(r)=1+\frac{QL^4}{2r^2}+o(r^{-2}),
\]

the slice has finite asymptotically flat 5D mass. In Tangherlini isotropic normalization `ψ=1+μ/(4r²)+…`, this coefficient corresponds to `μ=2QL⁴`; the associated ADM mass is `M=3Ω₃μ/(16πG₅)` with `Ω₃=2π²`, `κ₅²=8πG₅`. The exact charge convention assumes the usual asymptotically Cartesian normalization.

The areal radius of a round sphere is `R(r)=rψ(r)` and proper radial distance satisfies `dℓ=ψdr`; therefore

\[
\frac{dR}{d\ell}=1+\frac{r\psi'}{\psi}=1-\frac{I(r)}{r^2\psi(r)}.
\]

Writing `y=r/L`, `I/r²=QL²J(y)/y²`, `J(y)=\int_0^y x^5e^{-x²}dx`. For `y≤1`, `J(y)≤y⁶/6`; for `y≥1`, `J(y)≤1`. Thus `J(y)/y²≤1`, and since `ψ≥1`,

\[
QL^2<1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad dR/d\ell>0\quad\text{for all }r\ge0.
\]

This is a simple sufficient symbolic condition, not an optimized parameter bound. At a time-symmetric slice the future radial null expansions of round `S³` are `θ_±=±(3/R)dR/dℓ`; hence this condition excludes initially trapped and anti-trapped **round** spheres in the constructed spherical data. It is not a claim about non-round trapped surfaces or later evolution. There is no initial event horizon conclusion; an event horizon is global.

The scalar has a smooth nonzero gradient: these are not vacuum data. But `V=0` and monotone `φ` mean there is no double-well wall, no false-vacuum interior and no brane. The data are momentarily time-symmetric (`K=π_φ=0`), not demonstrated to be collapsing. Their subsequent fate—dispersion, collapse or other regular evolution—is **NOT_COMPUTED**. They serve only as a rigorous regular parent-like constraint example.

## 4. Локальная expansion против глобального рождения

### 4.1 Wall worldvolume

If a 5D shell is already present and timelike, its induced metric may be

\[
ds_\Sigma^2=-d\tau_{trans}^2+R(\tau_{trans})^2d\Omega_3^2,
\qquad H_\Sigma=\frac{\dot R}{R}.
\]

In the 5D spherical vacuum patches `f_±=1−μ_±/R²−Λ_±R²/6`, the 5D Israel equation for a 4D pure-tension wall is, in the declared normal convention,

\[
\epsilon_-\sqrt{\dot R^2+f_-}-\epsilon_+\sqrt{\dot R^2+f_+}
=\frac{\kappa_5^2\sigma R}{3}.
\]

The coefficient is `κ₅²/(5−2)`; it is not experiment #33’s 4D shell coefficient. Any squared effective potential must be checked back against this unsquared equation and both normal signs. A simple local bounce has `R_b>0`, `W(R_b)=0`, `W'(R_b)>0`, but that only changes the shell radius. Gravity localization, a daughter bulk interior and regular parent-only formation are further questions.

### 4.2 Raychaudhuri и причинность

For a hypersurface-orthogonal null congruence in 5D, whose screen dimension is `D−2=3`,

\[
\frac{d\theta}{d\lambda}=-\frac{\theta^2}{3}-\sigma_{AB}\sigma^{AB}-R_{AB}k^Ak^B.
\]

For canonical scalar, Einstein equations imply `R_AB k^A k^B=κ₅²(k·∇φ)²≥0` (cosmological constant drops out). A positive tension wall saturates its intrinsic surface NEC; distributional focusing must be handled by the junction. Under null convergence, a trapped compact `S³` plus the other global hypotheses of the higher-dimensional Penrose theorem implies future null geodesic incompleteness, not formation of a daughter. Incompleteness may indicate singularity or failure of the assumed extension; it is not a bounce proof.

If a new connected component is claimed to appear on later Cauchy slices while the full spacetime remains globally hyperbolic, the standard global property that Cauchy surfaces are diffeomorphic obstructs literal topology change. An effective domain or wall can form without changing manifold topology; a genuinely new component requires clarifying which regularity/global-hyperbolicity assumptions fail or adding new physics. No event horizon is established by the time-symmetric initial data above.

## 5. Родительский exterior и причинные часы

If a 5D asymptotic region is vacuum, spherically symmetric and solves ordinary 5D Einstein equations with a cosmological constant, its local exterior is Tangherlini–(A)dS with `f(R)=1−μ/R²−Λ₅R²/6`; only under those hypotheses is the mass parameter constant in that vacuum region. A scalar pulse reaching the exterior, 5D gravitational radiation, a brane, non-vacuum projected Weyl stress or changing boundary flux invalidates the simple vacuum argument. Conserved parent mass requires a defined asymptotic charge and controlled flux; it is neither necessarily lost nor guaranteed unchanged.

Two distinct setups must not be confused:

1. **P is a brane:** it is a pre-existing timelike hypersurface with its own induced metric, localized sources and Israel conditions. Its exterior 4D metric is not automatically Schwarzschild; bulk Weyl and KK data enter.
2. **P is a bulk region:** P and any candidate D live in one 5D manifold, with bulk causal cones and one global initial-value problem. The parent observer is a specified 5D worldline or brane observer; there is no second local universe by notation alone.

Clock labels are fixed as `T_P` (proper time along a chosen P observer), `τ_trans` (proper time along a specified wall/transition trajectory), `t_D` (proper time of a homogeneous D-brane FLRW congruence, only if it exists), and `t_5` (bulk coordinate). For a brane `dτ_i²=−h^{(i)}_{ab}dx_i^adx_i^b/c²` when coordinates have length units; in `c=1` units the `1/c²` is omitted. A map between `T_P` and `t_D` requires a specified emission event, bulk causal path or mode, reception event, observer worldlines and clock protocol. Different rays/modes yield different delays. `dt_D/dT_P`, `K0a_D`, and the static RS1 `e^{-kL}` ratio are not interchangeable; the last applies to a pre-existing static Killing background, not a newborn dynamic daughter.

## 6. Теоремы: что ограничено, а что остаётся открытым

- **Farhi–Guth (1987):** the locally cited result concerns a particular spherical 4D false-vacuum bubble, asymptotically flat parent and NEC assumptions with growth beyond a critical size. Its premises are not automatically theorems for a 5D scalar wall or a compact/AdS bulk. A 5D analog needs a new derivation of energy condition, asymptotics, global hyperbolicity and topology. Applicability here is **NOT_DERIVED**.
- **Penrose:** requires a suitable trapped surface, null convergence/focusing, and global assumptions such as a noncompact Cauchy surface and global hyperbolicity in the chosen theorem version. It concludes causal geodesic incompleteness; it does not say that a child region forms. For our analytic data only round initial expansions are checked, and they are untrapped under `QL²<1`.
- **Topological censorship:** the local reference assumes global/asymptotic conditions and averaged null energy. It constrains causal communication/topology relative to infinity; it is not a universal ban on hidden interiors or all higher-dimensional brane configurations. Check the actual bulk completion before use.
- **Cosmic censorship:** conjectural/global and not assumed. A trapped surface, apparent horizon or coordinate horizon is not by itself an event horizon or daughter boundary.
- **Quantum nucleation:** different from classical Cauchy evolution; no tunneling action, instanton or probability is computed.

No blanket 5D no-go is claimed. Equally, lack of a directly applicable theorem is not evidence that formation succeeds.

## 9. Один следующий аналитический вопрос

**Ровно один следующий шаг:** задать smooth false-vacuum потенциал и regular spherical 5D parent-only Cauchy data с полностью решёнными constraints, затем аналитически установить, допускают ли domain-wall-forming perturbations регулярную timelike level set `Φ=Φ_*` до возникновения trapped `S³`/геодезической неполноты при NEC. До этого шага определить допустимые asymptotics и точный boundary value problem; стоп-критерии — отсутствие constraints-compatible data в заявленном классе, немедленная потеря timelike/nondegenerate wall, либо выполнение применимого focusing obstruction. Не запускать solver и не называть wall дочерней вселенной без отдельной глобальной causal construction.

## 10. Ограничения этой записки

Приведена аналитическая constrained initial-data семья, но не выполнены 5D evolution, wall formation, apparent/event horizon search, global geodesic completion, stability/ghost spectrum или causal signal tracing. Параметры остались символическими, никаких численных диапазонов/вероятностей не оценивалось. Результат — существование допустимого parent-like среза и отделение проверяемых утверждений; **эмпирическое установление H1 отсутствует**.
