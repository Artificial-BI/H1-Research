# H1 v0.2 — минимальный общий фундаментальный гравитационный сектор

## 1. Краткий вывод

Рассмотрены три разных устройства связи: (1) две локализованные 4D области в общем 5D bulk, (2) Hassan–Rosen (HR) bimetric gravity на одном 4D многообразии и (3) отдельные 4D многообразия с явно заданной интерфейсной correspondence. Они отвечают на разные вопросы и не сводятся к одной готовой теории.

Наиболее определённый action-level scaffold для *гравитационного влияния между различными индуцированными метриками* — общий bulk с динамическими brane embeddings и локализованной материей. Он задаёт причинный перенос через bulk и может дать разные induced metrics/proper times. Но это модель областей в одном более высокоразмерном пространстве-времени; она **не доказывает** образование независимой дочерней вселенной и не выводит переход чёрная дыра → bounce. Если H1 требует двух изначально несвязанных 4D многообразий, brane-модель не удовлетворяет этому требованию без дополнительного глобального построения.

HR даёт явное взаимодействие двух динамических метрик, но только после отождествления точек на общем базовом многообразии. Это общий метрический сектор, а не связь между двумя manifold без correspondence. Третья конструкция показывает, как такая correspondence может быть введена через общий timelike интерфейс, однако её граничная динамика, DOF и ghost/hyperbolicity свойства здесь **не проверены**. При независимой диффеоморфной инвариантности и без bridge/map/bulk взаимодействие отсутствует.

Ни одна архитектура не выводит все три предпосылки H1 одновременно: (a) nonsingular bounce внутри astrophysical BH, (b) динамическое образование глобально отличной дочерней геометрии, (c) физический общий gravity sector. В частности, `dt_D/dT_P` остаётся **NOT_DERIVED**; повторные рождения не рассматриваются.

## 2. Обозначения и статусы

Используется сигнатура `(-,+,+,+,...)`, `c=ℏ=1`. В `D` измерениях `[κ_D²]=L^(D−2)`, `[Λ_D]=L^−2`; для 4D reduced Planck mass `[M_4]=L^−1`, `κ_4²=M_4^−2`. Тензоры материи определены вариацией `T_AB=−2/√−g δS_m/δg^{AB}`. У brane `p`-пространственности её натяжение/локальный лагранжиан имеет размерность `L^−(p+1)`.

Метки в этом документе:

- **VERIFIED_WITHIN_MODEL** — тождество следует из явно заданной структуры действия/геометрии; это не эмпирическая проверка H1.
- **CONDITIONAL** — вывод верен только при перечисленных симметриях, граничных данных, ветви или приближении.
- **NOT_DERIVED** — нужное уравнение/механизм из выбранной структуры не следует.
- **CONFLICTING** — требование несовместимо с заявленной архитектурой, пока не добавлена новая структура.

`T_P` обозначает собственное время выбранного родительского наблюдателя, `tau_B` — время перехода/интерфейсной мировой поверхности, `t_D` — космическое собственное время выбранной дочерней FLRW congruence. `u,v` — параметры нулевых координат, не часы по определению. Это разные параметры и часы.

После выключения связи две локальные GR-космологии могут существовать независимо, но их начальные данные остаются независимыми. Никакое равенство законов не создаёт корреляцию событий или времени.

## 4. I. Общий bulk и brane-localized matter

### 4.1 Действие и локальные уравнения

Для двух timelike 4D гиперповерхностей `Σ_i` в 5D bulk допустим минимальный архитектурный набор

\[
S=\frac{1}{2\kappa_5^2}\int_{\mathcal B} d^5X\sqrt{-G}(R_5-2\Lambda_5)
+\frac{1}{\kappa_5^2}\sum_i\int_{\Sigma_i}d^4x\sqrt{-h_i}\,K_i
+\sum_i\left[-\lambda_i\int_{\Sigma_i}\sqrt{-h_i}+S_{m,i}[h_i,\psi_i]\right].
\]

GHY term written here assumes boundary pieces and outward-normal conventions are treated consistently; at an orbifold/fixed brane the usual one-sided/jump factors must be fixed before varying. `G_AB` is the bulk metric; `X_i^A(x)` are dynamical embeddings; `h_{i μν}=G_{AB}(X_i)∂_μX_i^A∂_νX_i^B` is the induced metric; `K_{i μν}` its extrinsic curvature; `λ_i` is a 4D brane tension with `[λ_i]=L^−4`; `S_{m,i}` contains fields confined to that brane. `[κ_5²]=L³` and `[K]=L^−1`.

Bulk variation gives

\[
G_{AB}^{(5)}+\Lambda_5G_{AB}=\kappa_5^2\left(T_{AB}^{bulk}+\sum_i S_{i AB}\,\delta_{\Sigma_i}\right),
\]

where `S_i` is the localized surface stress tensor and `δ_Σ` is normalized with the chosen transverse proper distance. On an actual two-sided junction with a declared normal orientation, the Israel equation is

\[
[K_{\mu\nu}-Kh_{\mu\nu}]=-\kappa_5^2 S_{\mu\nu}.
\]

The sign/jump convention matters; this equation is not a shell trajectory until a bulk solution, embeddings and initial/boundary data are supplied. Variation with respect to `X_i` also gives the normal force/embedding equation, relating jumps of bulk normal stress to brane stress and extrinsic curvature. It cannot be omitted if branes move.

Tangential projection of the bulk Bianchi identity (Codazzi) gives, under the same convention,

\[
D_\mu S^\mu{}_{\nu}=-[T_{AB}^{bulk}n^A h^B{}_{\nu}].
\]

Thus brane matter alone is conserved when there is no tangential bulk energy-momentum flux. Otherwise its balance law includes a derived flux; one must not subtract a notional “daughter mass” from parent ADM mass without a common conserved charge and specified boundary. Total diffeomorphism invariance gives bulk-plus-boundary identities, not a universal separate 4D energy conservation across two cosmologies.

### 4.2 Clocks, causal propagation and what it does not imply

For a brane observer `x_i^μ(λ)`,

\[
d\tau_i^2=-h_{i\mu\nu}dx_i^\mu dx_i^\nu.
\]

Bulk null/timelike propagation is governed by `G_AB`; brane matter signals are governed by the induced `h_i` cones. A physical cross-brane timing statement requires a bulk causal curve and a specified emission event, reception event, brane observers and clock normalization. Only then can one compare elapsed `T_P` and `t_D` on paired worldline segments. `tau_B` may parametrize a brane/interface trajectory but is not either cosmological clock. The scale-factor formula `dt_D/dT_P=K0 a_D` does not follow from the action.

The branes can have distinct induced geometries, but are embedded in one common causal bulk. A brane-localized Big-Bang-like phase requires an actual regular bulk/brane solution and global initial data. A critical BH interior, nonsingular bounce, second asymptotic end, or daughter birth is **NOT_DERIVED** from the action architecture alone. Low-energy 4D GR is conditional on compactification/warping, boundary conditions and any radion stabilization; no numerical regime is asserted here.

**Вердикт I:** bulk Einstein equations and Israel/Codazzi relations are **VERIFIED_WITHIN_MODEL** once boundary conventions are fixed; separate brane matter conservation is **CONDITIONAL** on zero bulk flux; BH-triggered daughter formation and a clock map are **NOT_DERIVED**. If “daughter” must be a wholly disconnected 4D spacetime rather than a brane-domain in shared 5D, this architecture is **CONFLICTING** with that stricter ontology.

## 5. II. Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity

### 5.1 Действие, источники и Bianchi identities

On a single differentiable 4D manifold `M`, a schematic HR action is

\[
S=\frac{M_g^2}{2}\int_M\sqrt{-g}R[g]
+\frac{M_f^2}{2}\int_M\sqrt{-f}R[f]
-m_*^2M_{eff}^2\int_M\sqrt{-g}\sum_{n=0}^4\beta_n e_n(S)
+S_{m,g}[g,\psi_g]+S_{m,f}[f,\psi_f],\qquad S=\sqrt{g^{-1}f}.
\]

Here `e_n` are elementary symmetric polynomials of the matrix square root, `β_n` are dimensionless, `[M_g]=[M_f]=[M_eff]=[m_*]=L^−1`. Both metrics are nondegenerate Lorentzian tensors on the same points; a real, admissible matrix square-root branch and compatible causal backgrounds are required. The displayed potential is the special HR/dRGT structure; a generic function of two metrics does not inherit its constraint result.

Metric variation has the form

\[
M_g^2G_{\mu\nu}[g]+m_*^2M_{eff}^2V^{(g)}_{\mu\nu}=T^{(g)}_{\mu\nu},\qquad
M_f^2G_{\mu\nu}[f]+m_*^2M_{eff}^2V^{(f)}_{\mu\nu}=T^{(f)}_{\mu\nu},
\]

where `V^(g),V^(f)` are the metric variations of the same interaction potential, with determinant factors absorbed into their definitions. For separately minimally coupled matter actions, matter equations imply `∇_g^μT^(g)_{μν}=0` and `∇_f^μT^(f)_{μν}=0`. The interaction tensors are not independent external sources: the diagonal diffeomorphism identity relates their divergences and imposes the bimetric Bianchi constraint. In homogeneous ansätze this becomes a branch/lapse/scale-factor relation; one cannot choose both scale factors and lapses arbitrarily.

The special potential supplies the nonlinear constraint eliminating the Boulware–Deser extra mode in the intended theory. It does **not** guarantee stability of every FLRW/black-hole background, absence of Higuchi/gradient/vector instabilities, a well-posed common initial value problem, or safety of arbitrary matter coupled simultaneously to both metrics. Generic double-metric matter couplings can reintroduce an unwanted degree of freedom (at least as an EFT problem); separate matter sectors are the conservative minimal choice.

### 5.2 Разные метрики, предел GR и отсутствие автоматического рождения

The action gives two path-dependent line elements on common events:

\[
d\tau_g^2=-g_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu dx^\nu,\qquad d\tau_f^2=-f_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu dx^\nu,
\]

only along curves timelike in the respective metric. This is not a universal conversion between proper times: curves, observer fields and event correspondence must be physically selected. Causal cones may differ, so a common time function/global hyperbolicity and the allowed signal paths need a solution-specific check.

As `m_*→0`, the two Einstein–Hilbert sectors decouple but remain fields on the **same base manifold**. If each sector is separately minimally coupled, this is a valid limit of two local GR dynamics with independently chosen initial data; the coupling then predicts no cross-sector gravitational influence. A single-metric observational GR limit additionally requires specifying which metric couples to observed matter and how the other sector decouples or is consistently hidden. Conversely, nonzero HR potential transmits metric influence only between points already identified by the common base. It creates neither a new component of `M` nor a topological throat by itself.

For a homogeneous `g`-sector bounce, `H_g=(1/N_g) da_g/(a_g dt)=0`, `dH_g/dτ_g>0` and finite invariants are necessary, but the `f` equation and Bianchi branch must hold simultaneously. There is no sign-only universal bounce criterion for arbitrary `β_n`. A black-hole exterior remains Schwarzschild only in a region where the relevant equations reduce to 4D vacuum GR with spherical symmetry; extra fields, nonvacuum stress, or interaction terms invalidate that inference.

**Вердикт II:** common-manifold bimetric interaction and its diagonal-diffeomorphism constraints are **VERIFIED_WITHIN_MODEL** for the HR potential and stated matter coupling. Healthy cosmology is **CONDITIONAL**. A disconnected pair `M_P ⊔ M_D` with no point identification cannot carry the pointwise `e_n(√g⁻¹f)` potential: that demand is **CONFLICTING** until a map/interface/bulk is added. Bounce, topology change and `dt_D/dT_P` remain **NOT_DERIVED**.

## 6. III. Независимые 4D многообразия и bridge/interface

### 6.1 Default: независимость означает отсутствие связи

For disjoint manifolds with independent diffeomorphism groups,

\[
S_0=S_P[g_P,\psi_P]+S_D[g_D,\psi_D],\qquad
\delta_{\xi_P}S_P=0,\quad \delta_{\xi_D}S_D=0,
\]

contains no cross-variation and yields `G_P=κ_P T_P`, `G_D=κ_D T_D` (or the respective local equations). There is no canonical operation comparing tensor components at unrelated points, no shared propagating field and no cross-force. Equality of constants or identical equations does not alter this. A bilocal term `∫_{M_P}∫_{M_D} K(x,y)...` is not yet a theory: it needs a covariant kernel, measures, field transformations, causal support, variation rules and a consistent Ward identity for each diffeomorphism.

### 6.2 Explicit interface candidate (a new, unvalidated theory)

One way to make a coupling invariant without identifying all interior points is to introduce a common abstract timelike 3-manifold `Σ` and embeddings `X_i:Σ→∂M_i` into timelike boundaries. Pullback metrics are

\[
q^{(i)}_{ab}=g^{(i)}_{\mu\nu}(X_i)\,\partial_aX_i^\mu\partial_bX_i^\nu.
\]

An illustrative action is

\[
S_{int}=\int_\Sigma d^3y\sqrt{-q}\left[\frac{M_\Sigma}{2}R[q]-\lambda_\Sigma\right]
+\frac12\sum_{i=P,D}\int_\Sigma d^3y\sqrt{-q}\,\Lambda_i^{ab}(q^{(i)}_{ab}-q_{ab}),
\]

added to separate 4D Einstein–Hilbert + GHY + matter actions on `M_P,M_D`. Here `q` is an auxiliary Lorentzian interface metric, `Λ_i^{ab}` are symmetric Lagrange multipliers, `[M_Σ]=L^−1`, `[λ_Σ]=[Λ_i]=L^−3` when `y^a` have length units and metric components are dimensionless. Multiplier variation enforces `q^(P)=q=q^(D)` on the interface; variation of `q`, embeddings and the bulk metrics produces interface stress and boundary momentum conditions. The relative signs depend on the two outward-normal conventions and must be obtained from the complete GHY/boundary variation, not guessed from a single Israel jump formula.

The construction is invariant under independent bulk diffeomorphisms when each embedding transforms as a map, and under reparametrizations of `Σ`. It gives a common event correspondence **only on `Σ`**. The surface action can carry its own stress/curvature response; the 4D bulk equations acquire boundary conditions rather than an arbitrary pointwise force at interior points. If one actually identifies the two boundaries as one spacetime boundary and glues their normal directions, the quotient may become a connected stratified spacetime; that topology and causal atlas must be specified. Keeping two interiors merely “in contact” through `Σ` is another global construction, not automatic from the action.

This toy is deliberately not called ghost-free or complete. It introduces at least `q`, two embeddings and multiplier constraints in addition to both metrics and matter. Constraint classification, whether `q` carries 2+1D modes, boundary well-posedness, hyperbolicity, sign of induced kinetic terms, and radiative stability are **NOT_COMPUTED**. `M_Σ>0` is a necessary positive-sign choice for the displayed intrinsic Einstein kinetic term in its ordinary 2+1D regime, not a sufficient stability theorem for the coupled boundary system. A controlled decoupling limit is to remove the interface action/constraints (or take its coupling to zero); then each bulk returns to its independent GR sector and all cross-predictions disappear.

**Вердикт III:** zero coupling of independent diffeomorphism-invariant actions is **VERIFIED_WITHIN_MODEL**. The displayed interface is a covariant *candidate ansatz* (**CONDITIONAL** on boundary variation and well-posedness), not evidence of a viable mediator. It does not define a bulk event map away from `Σ`, a birth mechanism, or a daughter cosmological clock. If a physical bridge must transmit signals through a region rather than impose interface boundary response, a separate dynamical bulk mediator is needed; that returns to architecture I or a new theory.

## 7. Сохранение, causal cones, устойчивость и наблюдаемость

### 7.1 Локальные законы и общий баланс

For each minimally coupled matter sector, its own field equations and local diffeomorphism identity give covariant conservation in that sector when it exchanges no matter with a mediator. With coupling, the combined Noether identity includes mediator/interface stress and boundary flux. There is no generally meaningful scalar “total mass of both universes” unless a common spacetime, asymptotic symmetry and boundary charge have been defined. In a 5D model one may define bulk charges at specified boundaries; in HR each metric has its own geometric charges subject to common constraints; for disjoint manifolds the two charges are independent at zero coupling.

### 7.2 Symbolic gates, not invented numerical ranges

- **Bulk:** `κ_5²>0`; Lorentzian nondegenerate `G_AB`; timelike brane pullbacks `det h_i<0` in the chosen signature; consistent outward normals and boundary conditions; positive-sign Einstein kinetic term. These are structural gates, not sufficient no-ghost/hyperbolicity conditions for a compactified spectrum.
- **HR:** `M_g²>0`, `M_f²>0`, real chosen matrix square root, nondegenerate Lorentzian metrics, `m_*²≥0` in the conventional parameterization. No sign-only bounds on `β_n` guarantee stability; lapse constraints and perturbation kinetic/gradient matrices must be evaluated on each solution. A real root alone does not prove compatible causal cones.
- **Interface:** nondegenerate Lorentzian `q,q_i`, smooth embeddings of full rank, compatible time orientation on both pullbacks, and a well-posed boundary-value problem. The sign of every propagating kinetic eigenvalue must be derived after constraints; positive `M_Σ` alone is insufficient.
- **All models:** characteristic cones of each propagating mode must be real and hyperbolic on the background. Cross-sector causal influence exists only along those cones and only if a globally defined causal path/interaction support exists.

Empirical gates are model-specific: deviations in gravitational-wave propagation/speed, extra polarizations, equivalence-principle violations, fifth forces, binary dynamics and cosmological expansion are constrained observationally in our sector. This document quotes **no numerical bounds**, because those bounds depend on the coupling, screening, background and dataset and no source review was performed here. Observations of our sector constrain a proposed coupling only after a definite action maps its parameters to the observables.

### 7.3 Parent black-hole mass and horizons

In ordinary 4D vacuum GR with spherical symmetry, Birkhoff’s theorem makes each connected vacuum exterior locally Schwarzschild; its mass parameter is constant in that vacuum region. It does not establish that a daughter forms. If the added sector carries stress, radiation, boundary flux or extra metric degrees of freedom, the hypotheses fail: an exterior mass function/horizon can respond, but it need not change by a universal amount or even monotonically. No architecture here implies “mass lost into daughter” or “parent mass exactly conserved.” The charge, surface and flux law must be derived from the selected complete action and global boundary conditions.

## 8. Перенос формул из реестра и экспериментов

- **#31:** identical daughter-only FLRW predictions for `K0 a_D` and constant `K0` are verified in the isolated-metric toy. This carries as a warning against mistaking a time relabeling for an observable. It does not constrain a real mediator, because no cross-sector interaction was present. `dt_D/dT_P=K0 a_D` remains **NOT_DERIVED**.
- **#32:** `H=0`, `dot H>0`, `a_b>0`, finite invariants are local bounce tests for the specified FLRW equations. The closed illustrative solution had disconnected allowed branches separated by `F(a)<0`. Those inequalities may be reused only after deriving the effective stress/field equations of the selected bulk or interface theory; no `#32` matter/radiation background can be grafted into a vacuum shell or HR action without derivation.
- **#33–35:** Israel equations and unsquared sign checks transfer only when an actual codimension-one hypersurface, induced metric, stress tensor and normal conventions are part of the chosen common-manifold/bulk action. They do not provide a generic bridge law between disjoint manifolds. Shell proper time `tau_B`, static patch coordinates and `t_D` remain distinct.
- **#36:** canonical-scalar NEC/bounce limitations apply to that minimally coupled Einstein-scalar model and its flat-FLRW assumptions; they are not automatically statements about torsion, bulk gravity or HR interactions.
- **#37:** global causal classification still requires a specified manifold, Cauchy data, null cones, embeddings and causal domain. A local bridge action does not settle event horizons.
- **#38:** equality/inheritance toy inputs were imposed and were not identifiable from daughter-only observables. This remains a caution against treating shared constants as evidence of a physical common gravitational sector.
- **#39–#41F, отчёт о тестах коллапса–121:** these scalar/GR numerical backends do not validate any of the three candidate theories. Their numerical status is separate from the action-level identities above. ## 9. Предварительный выбор и серьёзное возражение

**Provisional scaffold:** use a 5D Einstein bulk with two matter-localizing timelike hypersurfaces as the first architecture to formulate if H1 permits “distinct local universes” to mean distinct brane-induced geometries inside a shared higher-dimensional causal spacetime. It has an explicit propagating mediator, pullback metrics, variational embedding data and local junction/conservation laws; it avoids an arbitrary bilocal kernel.

**Серьёзное возражение:** это может отвергать самую сильную онтологическую часть H1. Две brane не являются двумя manifold, которые независимо возникают из родительской чёрной дыры; глобальный bulk уже постулирует их embedding, а влияние идёт по обычным higher-dimensional causal curves. Без решения bulk initial-value problem нельзя говорить ни о causal isolation, ни о новом Big-Bang-like domain. Если владелец требует именно несвязанные 4D многообразия, provisional choice меняется на интерфейсный кандидат III как вопрос для отдельной action/constraint-проверки, но он существенно менее разработан и пока не имеет установленной ghost freedom.

HR — fallback, если важнее две динамические метрики, чем раздельные manifold: принять общее базовое `M` открыто и не называть метрики родителем/дочерью без глобального causal construction. ECSK spin-torsion может быть рассмотрен отдельно как bounce-кандидат из предыдущего сравнения, но его алгебраический torsion не даёт дальнодействующей cross-geometry связи. Ни один из этих элементов не следует складывать в гибрид без новой общей вариационной задачи.

## 10. Что ещё требуется, чтобы модель стала математически полной

Для любого выбранного кандидата необходимо до численной работы:

1. Зафиксировать топологию, manifold(s), signature, единицы, action, boundary terms, все динамические поля и допустимые конфигурации.
2. Варьировать полный bulk/brane/interface action, включая embedding и boundary variables; получить constraints, Bianchi/Noether identities и материальные balance laws с однозначными нормалями.
3. Выбрать регулярные parent Cauchy data и boundary/asymptotic conditions; проверить constraints и существование хорошо поставленной IVP с данным causal cone.
4. Определить критерий дочернего региона: не полевая область или bounce по отдельности, а регулярная эволюция, явная глобальная геометрия, null reachability относительно заданных parent observers/infinity и физически определённое causal separation.
5. Вывести background bounce criterion в выбранном действии, затем проверить shear/inhomogeneous perturbations, kinetic signs, gradients, characteristic speeds, ghost constraints и область EFT.
6. Определить, какие matter поля живут на каждой геометрии, какие заряды локальны, какой flux передаёт mediator и какое наблюдение в parent может его проверить.
7. Только после определения event correspondence определить `T_P`, `tau_B`, `t_D` и проверить, является ли отношение elapsed proper times локально вычислимым. `K0 a_D` не подставлять как аксиому, если задача — вывести mapping.
8. Проверить GR/decoupling limits и сопоставить параметризованные signatures с данными; не переносить ограничения между архитектурами без явного преобразования параметров.

## 11. Один ограниченный следующий аналитический тест

Для рекомендованного bulk scaffold выполнить **одну линеаризованную проверку вокруг плоского 5D bulk с двумя параллельными, не искривлёнными branes**, без космологического bounce: выписать полный quadratic action для tensor perturbations, boundary/junction conditions и brane-to-brane retarded Green function. Заранее принять gates: положительные kinetic residues для распространяющихся мод, гиперболические bulk characteristics, конечная causal support внутри bulk cone и явный предел исчезновения межbrane ответа при выбранном decoupling/boundary limit. Это проверит, что заявленный “общий gravity sector” действительно передаёт допустимое возмущение и не вводится словами. Тест не проверяет образование дочерней вселенной; если базовая линейная система не проходит, bounce/formation не начинать.

## 12. Источники и статус библиографии

Локальный список [references.md](../../references.md) содержит Israel (1966), Farhi–Guth (1987), Farhi–Guth–Guven (1990), BGV, Penrose, topological censorship и k-essence. Они относятся к заявленным там условиям и не подтверждают этот общий H1 механизм. Предыдущее [сравнение кандидатов](h1-v0.2-candidate-theories-comparison.md) фиксирует Popławski/BGG как данные, предоставленные владельцем, но не сверенные по локальным копиям.

Локальной копии первичных работ по HR/brane в проекте нет. Для навигации указываются библиографические записи, известные автору, но не проверенные по локальному первоисточнику в этой задаче: S. F. Hassan, R. A. Rosen, “Bimetric Gravity from Ghost-free Massive Gravity,” *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 108, 041101 (2012), DOI `10.1103/PhysRevLett.108.041101`; L. Randall, R. Sundrum, “A Large Mass Hierarchy from a Small Extra Dimension,” *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 83, 3370 (1999), DOI `10.1103/PhysRevLett.83.3370`; “An Alternative to Compactification,” *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 83, 4690 (1999), DOI `10.1103/PhysRevLett.83.4690`. Эти записи даны как библиографические ориентиры с пометкой **не сверено офлайн**, а не как проверка заявленных теорем. Формы действий выше — архитектурные записи для сравнения и дальнейшего вывода; это не доказательство стабильности конкретной модели и не законченная теория H1. Интернет/внешние источники не использовались.
